# Bluebell Pilates Website Brief

## Project

Create a modern, responsive single-page website to replace an existing Instabook landing page.

The design should feel calm, welcoming and premium, reflecting a boutique Pilates studio.

The site will be hosted on GitHub Pages as a static website.

---

## Objectives

- Clearly explain who Bluebell Pilates is.
- Showcase the studio through high-quality photography.
- Encourage visitors to enquire or book.
- Work beautifully on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Fast loading and accessible.

---

## Target Audience

Adults looking for Pilates classes, including:

- Beginners
- Existing Pilates practitioners
- People recovering from injury
- People wanting to improve strength, posture and wellbeing

---

## Design Style

Keywords:

- Clean
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Calm
- Natural
- Spacious

Use generous white space and large photography.

Avoid clutter.

Subtle animations are encouraged.

### 4 Classes

Title "One to One Reformer Sessions with Shelley"

Text
The beauty of this exercise system is it focuses on strengthening the core and balance in a controlled way that is suitable for rehabilitation after injury (which is how the system was developed) to advanced level exercises that trained athletes will find challenging.

Pre and Post Natal: The reformer Pilates system is an excellent way to help your body before and after childbirth. You are very welcome to bring your baby to sessions

Any Age: The Pilates Reformer can support as well as challenge , and all levels of fitness and ages can benefit.  Many people start in Reformer Pilates in retirement, discovering the amazing benefits, and are still enjoying it into their 90s!

Modern day life and long hours at a desk can tighten up our shoulders and neck, hunching our posture. The exercises on the  Pilates Reformer  will stretch you out, engage your core and leave you relaxed and refreshed.

The Reformer bed equipment is designed to support and uses springs in a really clever way, gradually increasing intensity in some exercises and supporting in others, so working at lots of different levels, no matter your age, size or shape.
