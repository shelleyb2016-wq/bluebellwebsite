About Bluebell Pilates

A calm, welcoming studio for every body

I qualified as a mat Pilates instructor back in 2014 and enjoyed teaching classes, one-to-ones and small groups.

I then discovered Reformer Pilates which I love with a passion! I extended my training and qualifications and continue to learn each day. Each client is different, how they may feel that particular day is different and no session is ever the same. I now teach one-to-one sessions in my boutique home studio in Sharpthorne, West Sussex.

I love working with clients of all ages and abilities and the challenge of developing a training programme to meet each individual's unique needs and goals in an environment that is both stimulating and relaxing.

Classes

One to One Reformer Sessions with Shelley

The beauty of this exercise system is it focuses on strengthening the core and balance in a controlled way that is suitable for rehabilitation after injury — which is how the system was developed — through to advanced level exercises that trained athletes will find challenging.

The Reformer bed equipment is designed to support and uses springs in a really clever way, gradually increasing intensity in some exercises and supporting in others — working at lots of different levels, no matter your age, size or shape.

Pre & Post Natal

The Reformer Pilates system is an excellent way to help your body before and after childbirth. You are very welcome to bring your baby to sessions.

Any Age

The Reformer can support as well as challenge. Many people start in retirement, discovering the amazing benefits, and are still enjoying it into their 90s!

Posture & Relief

Long hours at a desk can tighten shoulders and neck and hunch our posture. The exercises stretch you out, engage your core and leave you relaxed and refreshed.

All Levels

From gentle rehabilitation to challenging athletic work, every programme is tailored to your unique needs, goals and how you feel that day.
